About Box Puzzle

A Sokoban-style Angular game where the player pushes boxes to target cells.

Standalone components, routing, guard, interceptor, services, HttpClient, Reactive Forms, ngOnInit, Input and Output are used.

Navbar, cards, buttons, forms, table, alerts, badges, breadcrumbs, button groups, list groups, progress bars, spinners, pagination, accordion and tabs are included.
